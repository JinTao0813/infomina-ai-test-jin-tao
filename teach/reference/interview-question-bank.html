Interview question bank

Answer aloud in 30–60 seconds. Direct answer first, one supporting fact second, boundary last.

How to practise: choose six questions from different sections. Rework answers beyond 90 seconds. Ask the teaching agent to select follow-ups, interrupt vague language, and score evidence versus overclaiming.

Brief, scope, and architecture

  1. What did the assignment actually ask you to demonstrate?
  2. Why did you choose TSMC instead of EPFL?
  3. Walk me through the solution end to end.
  4. Why put reusable logic outside the notebook?
  5. Why build a website and prototype beyond the required notebook?
  6. Which artifact is authoritative?
  7. What runs without FastAPI or raw files?
  8. What is production-oriented, prototyped, and still missing?

Data and preparation

  1. Where did row/user/venue/category counts come from?
  2. Why Latin-1, headerless parsing, and explicit dtypes?
  3. Why remove only exact duplicates?
  4. Why is (city, user_id) essential?
  5. Why derive local time from city rather than supplied offset?
  6. Why derive context before concatenating city frames?
  7. Why retain Home (private) in analysis but exclude it from candidates?
  8. How do venue metadata conflicts affect the analysis?

Entropy and implementation

  1. Explain Shannon entropy without a formula.
  2. Derive it for counts [3,1].
  3. Raw versus normalized entropy?
  4. Why normalized entropy zero for one location?
  5. What does entropy not measure?
  6. State the exact metric API.
  7. Walk the vectorized intermediate tables.
  8. Why report observation and unique-location counts?
  9. What is the complexity and scaling path?
  10. Which metric edge case would you improve?

Analysis and inference

  1. What is the headline result?
  2. Why median and IQR?
  3. Why rank correlations?
  4. Why pair weekday/weekend within user?
  5. Why require 20 observations per context?
  6. What exactly does the bootstrap interval mean?
  7. Why calculate category entropy?
  8. What do relative cohorts add—and risk?
  9. What did excluding Home (private) show?
  10. Can you claim weekends cause exploration or generalize to residents?

Ranker and API

  1. What is the product hypothesis?
  2. Calculate applied discovery for routine/weekend/balanced.
  3. Why does explicit intent dominate?
  4. What does confidence below 0.50 do?
  5. How do profile entropy, candidate novelty, and category familiarity differ?
  6. Derive a candidate novelty score.
  7. Why raise applied discovery to 1.5?
  8. Why sort exact scores before rounding?
  9. Which ranking parameter is learned?
  10. What does “new” mean?
  11. Why is baseline relevance not true relevance?
  12. How do schemas and endpoints fail safely?

Artifacts, privacy, and frontend

  1. Are profiles and candidates both synthetic?
  2. How is the candidate catalog minimized?
  3. Do support thresholds guarantee anonymity?
  4. Why use pseudonyms rather than proper venue names?
  5. How does exporter safety/freshness work?
  6. Why keep ranking logic in Python rather than React?
  7. Which loading, error, sparse, and accessibility states exist?
  8. What do 36 Python and 6 frontend test cases prove?

Product validation and critique

  1. What did implementing Discovery Mode prove—and not prove?
  2. Design the randomized experiment, then name your first next improvement.
