Glossary

Plain definitions for the terms used in the project.

TermPrecise meaning here
Check-inA voluntarily recorded Foursquare event. Not continuous presence, dwell time, purchase, demand, or footfall.
LocationFor the primary metric, a venue_id; for semantic diversity, a category_id.
Shannon entropy−Σ p log₂p; variety plus evenness of a user's observed event distribution.
BitEntropy unit when logarithm base 2 is used.
Raw entropyEntropy in bits. Its maximum grows with the number of observed locations K.
Normalized entropyH / log₂(K), set to zero for K ≤ 1. Measures evenness relative to the user's observed repertoire.
Observed repertoireThe unique venues or categories appearing in this sample—not every place the person actually visits.
Exact duplicateA row identical across all eight raw source fields; removed to avoid double probability mass.
Composite user key(city, user_id). Bare IDs restart and overlap between city files.
Timezone-awareA timestamp carrying UTC or IANA timezone semantics, permitting correct city-local calendar derivation.
IQRInterquartile range, from the 25th to 75th percentile; robust spread around the median.
Rank correlationCorrelation of ranks; captures monotonic association without assuming a linear relationship. It is not causation.
Paired comparisonWeekend and weekday scores belong to the same user; analyze their within-user difference.
Bootstrap intervalPercentiles of medians from user-level deltas resampled with replacement. Quantifies sampling variability conditional on this observed sample.
Sensitivity analysisRecompute after a defensible alternative choice—in this case removing Home (private)—to test conclusion robustness.
Routine-/exploration-orientedRelative within-city quartile labels for observed check-ins, not personality traits.
ObservationalResearchers did not assign treatments or control check-in generation; causal claims are unsupported.
Synthetic profileOne of four fixed fictional check-in histories anchored to aggregate ranges. It is not a source user.
Profile entropyNormalized venue/category entropy attached to a synthetic observed history. It is never a venue score.
ConfidenceAn illustrative [0,1] profile input controlling trust in inferred history patterns. It is not learned or calibrated here.
Applied discoveryA bounded policy blend of explicit choice, confidence-adjusted profile diversity, and eligible weekend adjustment. Not a probability.
Aggregate popularityA venue's check-in-count percentile among eligible historical candidates.
Baseline relevanceAn illustrative transform of aggregate popularity, 0.35 + 0.60 × popularity. Not measured personal relevance.
Aggregate candidate noveltyOne minus aggregate popularity percentile. Means less common in the historical sample—not new, good, or current.
Category familiarityBinary indicator that the candidate category occurs in the selected synthetic profile history.
Novelty score0.65 × aggregate novelty + 0.35 × category discovery, where category discovery is one minus familiarity.
Neutral fallbackBelow 0.50 confidence, use 0.50 for the inferred profile portion and suppress weekend adjustment; explicit choice still operates.
Pseudonymous candidateA filtered aggregate historical venue record labeled by city/category/number with source identifiers removed.
Generated artifactCommitted safe JSON/assets deterministically exported from the already-executed notebook and tied to its hash.
Deterministic rankerA hand-designed repeatable policy with no fitted model or randomness.
Claim boundaryThe explicit line separating observed evidence, interpretation, hypothesis, implementation behavior, and unvalidated outcomes.
GuardrailA metric that protects against harms while optimizing the primary KPI.
Language discipline: say “observed check-in diversity,” not “mobility,” “personality,” or “how exploratory a city is.” Say profiles are synthetic, candidates are pseudonymous data-derived aggregates, and ranking is illustrative—not learned or validated.
