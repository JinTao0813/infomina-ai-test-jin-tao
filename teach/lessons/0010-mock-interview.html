Round 2 · Metric and data

Derive entropy for counts [3,1].
N=4; p=(0.75,0.25); H=−[0.75log₂0.75+0.25log₂0.25]≈0.811 bits. K=2 gives a one-bit maximum, so normalized H≈0.811.
What is the exact metric API and implementation?
calculate_location_entropy(data, group_cols, location_col). Validate keys; count group-location rows; transform counts to group totals; compute probabilities and −p log₂p components; aggregate counts/support/entropy; divide by log₂K for K>1. No weights or configurable base.
Why does identity include city?
IDs restart across files: 3,376 city-user keys collapse to 2,293 bare IDs, and all 1,083 NYC IDs occur in Tokyo's numeric space. Bare grouping would merge different people.

Round 3 · Analytical judgment

What is your main finding?
Median normalized venue entropy is similar—0.853 NYC and 0.860 Tokyo—with strongly overlapping IQRs. Within-city variation is more decision-relevant. Most eligible users have positive weekend deltas, while category entropy shows place and activity diversity differ.
Can you say weekends make people explore?
No. The within-user delta is descriptively positive for most eligible users, but weekend context was not randomized and the selected check-in process permits confounding.
What is the strongest analytical critique?
Voluntary check-ins are a selected, incomplete observation process, and entropy depends on history length. The 20-per-context threshold lacks sensitivity analysis; test stability across history lengths first.

Round 4 · Ranker

Calculate applied discovery for routine + weekend + balanced.
Inferred=.6(.72)+.4(.58)=.664. Confidence-adjusted=.9(.664)+.1(.5)=.6476. Applied=.7(.5)+.3(.6476)+.9(.04)=.58028, reported as .5803.
How is a candidate scored?
Category discovery is one minus binary familiarity. Novelty=.65×aggregate novelty+.35×category discovery. Curved novelty weight is applied-discovery^1.5. Final=(1−weight)×illustrative baseline relevance+weight×novelty. Sort exact score descending, ID ascending; round only output.
What does sparse fallback do?
Below 0.50 confidence, the inferred profile portion is fixed at neutral 0.50 and weekend adjustment is suppressed. Explicit preference still leads; balanced therefore yields applied discovery 0.50.
Is any ranking parameter learned?
No. Profiles, confidence, blend weights, threshold, popularity transform, and power curve are synthetic or hand-designed illustrative choices. Tests prove deterministic behavior, not optimality.

Round 5 · Architecture, privacy, product

Why can the analysis page run without FastAPI or raw files?
An offline exporter converts the already-executed notebook into committed safe JSON and static assets. Next.js renders that artifact; only Discovery Mode calls FastAPI.
Are profiles and candidates both synthetic?
No. Four profiles are synthetic fixed examples. Sixteen candidates are aggregates derived offline from actual historical venue records, then filtered, thresholded, rounded, stripped of source IDs/coordinates/times, and given pseudonymous city/category/number labels.
What did the prototype prove?
Only that the product hypothesis can be implemented transparently, safely bounded, and tested as deterministic behavior. It did not prove current venue validity, relevance, engagement lift, or user benefit.
How would you validate the product?
Randomize users between baseline and context-aware ranking over the same pool. Measure save or selection per impression; guard dismissals, exits, repetition, distance, latency, sparse-history harm, fairness, privacy, and trust.
