Stage A · Profile and intent determine applied discovery

I = 0.60V + 0.40C
A = 0.70E + 0.30Q + W
  • V: normalized venue entropy; C: normalized category entropy.
  • E: explicit choice—0.20 familiar, 0.50 balanced, 0.80 something new.
  • Q = confidence × I + (1 − confidence) × 0.50 for reliable profiles.
  • W = confidence × weekend_delta on weekends for reliable profiles; otherwise zero.
  • If confidence < 0.50, set Q=0.50 and W=0. Clamp A to [0,1].

Worked profile: routine + weekend + balanced

I=.60(.72)+.40(.58)=.6640
Q=.90(.6640)+.10(.50)=.6476
W=.90(.04)=.0360
A=.70(.50)+.30(.6476)+.0360=.58028

Changing familiar → balanced changes E by 0.30 and therefore A by exactly 0.21. That is why explicit intent is the strongest controllable input.

Stage B · Candidate signals determine novelty

D = 1 − familiarity
N = 0.65 × aggregate_novelty + 0.35 × D

Familiarity is binary membership in the selected synthetic profile's familiar categories. Aggregate novelty is the inverse of aggregate popularity in the historical candidate catalog. Neither is entropy.

Stage C · A curved blend gives the final score

α = A1.5
final = (1 − α) × baseline_relevance + α × N

The power curve keeps low-discovery settings more relevance-led while still allowing “something new” to reorder the shared pool. The code sorts unrounded final scores descending and candidate IDs ascending; it rounds only response fields.

Interview language: “Applied discovery” is a policy blend, not a probability. “Baseline relevance” is an illustrative popularity transform, not measured relevance. “New” means absent from the profile category history or less common in the old sample—not newly opened.

Why can two candidates share aggregate novelty but receive different novelty scores?
Category discovery contributes 35%; a category absent from the synthetic profile gets one, while a familiar category gets zero.
Why sort exact scores before rounding?
Two distinct scores may display identically after rounding. Sorting rounded values would create false ties and let the ID tie-break override real score differences.
